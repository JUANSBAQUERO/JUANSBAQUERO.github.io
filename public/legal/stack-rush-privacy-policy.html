Stack Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Stack Rush ("the App") is a hypercasual mobile game developed by Juan Sebastián Baquero Moreno ("the Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. No personal data collection

Stack Rush does not require account creation, login, or any form submitted by the player. The App does not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. Local game data

Your score, high score, level progress, and preferences (sound/vibration settings) are stored locally on your device only, using standard mobile app storage. This data is never transmitted to the Developer or any third party, and is deleted if you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Stack Rush displays ads through Google AdMob (interstitial and rewarded video ads). To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID, Apple IDFA)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App usage and interaction data related to ad delivery

This processing is performed by Google, not by the Developer directly. For details on how Google collects and uses this data, see Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob partner sites/apps

4. Consent for EEA / UK users

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Google's consent management platform (UMP) may present a consent form before any personalized ad is served, in compliance with GDPR.

5. Children's privacy

Stack Rush is rated for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect any personal data from children.

6. Data retention and deletion

Since all gameplay data is stored locally and never sent to the Developer, there is nothing to delete on request — uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data. Any data processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's own retention policies (see links above).

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to baqueromorenoj@gmail.com.
